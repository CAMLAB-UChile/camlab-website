Department of Mechanical Engineering · Universidad de Chile

Computational & Applied Mechanics Laboratory

CAMLAB develops and democratizes modern numerical technologies for engineering modeling, analysis and simulation, with emphasis on the mechanics of solids and structures — combining nodal integration, meshfree Galerkin methods and the Virtual Element Method into free and open-source software.
