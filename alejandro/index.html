I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Universidad de Chile, where I conduct research on computational solid mechanics and teach courses on statics, machine elements, finite elements, and advanced methods in computational solid mechanics. I am a Mechanical Engineer and hold a Ph.D. in Civil (Structural) Engineering. I possess 20+ years of experience in mechanical design, structural analysis, finite element analysis and design of large mining equipment, including structural and mechanical design of solid-liquid separation equipment. I serve as a Consulting Engineer in these fields at national and international engineering and mining companies.

Research Interests

My primary research interests are related to the formulation of numerical methods and their implementation for computational modeling and simulation of engineering solids and structures and for applications in real-time structural health monitoring. Current research work is on:

  • Low-order discretization methods for large deformation problems in solid mechanics
  • Implicit theory of elasticity
  • Nonlinear continuum theory of dislocations
  • Supervised learning methods for real-time structural health monitoring
  • Meshfree and Finite Element Methods
  • Virtual Element Methods
