Vemtor Server

VEMTOR is our new server. It is based on 2 x Intel® Xeon® E5-2699 v4 (dual) processors with 22 cores each (2 logical cores per physical), which yields 88 CPUs with a total of 256 GB of compute RAM.

Summary of technical specifications:

  • 2 x Intel® Xeon® E5-2699 v4 e 55 MB 2.20 GHz (Turbo 3.6 Ghz) Processors
  • 1 x Motherboard ASUS Z10PE WS LGA2011-v3 Intel C612 PCH DDR4
  • 2 x Cooler Master MasterLiquid 120 All-In-One Liquid Cooler for LGA2011-V3
  • 8 x Kingston ValueRAM KVR21R15D4/32 DDR4-2133 32GB
  • 1 x Samsung 960 EVO 250GB V-NAND M.2 2280 PCIe NVMe SSD
  • 1 x Western Digital 1TB WD10EZEX SATA3 64mb 7200rpm HDD
  • 1 x PNY Quadro P1000 4GB Video Card
